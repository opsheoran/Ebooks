About This Course

SAMPLING-301: Theory of Sample Surveys is a specialized course designed for students of statistics, covering the fundamental principles and advanced techniques of sampling from simple random designs to complex multi-stage and varying probability surveys.

Developed for students at CCS Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar, this e-book combines theoretical proofs with step-by-step practical derivations and interactive numerical problems.

Preface

This web-book "Theory of Sample Surveys" is designed as a comprehensive resource for students and practitioners of statistics. It covers the fundamental principles and advanced techniques of sampling, from simple random designs to complex multi-stage surveys.

The goal is to provide a rigorous mathematical treatment of sampling theory while maintaining a clear, intuitive understanding of the underlying concepts.
