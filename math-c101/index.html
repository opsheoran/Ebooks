MATH-C101: Linear Algebra and Matrix Theory

Core Course Manual · B.Sc. Physical Science
4 Credits (4+0) Duration: 4 Months Dr. Manju Singh Tonk

Course Objectives & Learning Outcomes

Course Objectives:

  • To familiarize students with vector spaces, subspaces, and linear transformations.
  • To develop high competence in matrix operations, rank reduction, canonical forms, and systems of linear equations.
  • To establish the explicit relationship between linear transformations and their matrix representations.
  • To introduce eigenvalue problems, diagonalizability criteria, and quadratic forms with physical science applications.

Learning Outcomes: Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

  • Identify, construct, and analyze vector spaces, subspaces, quotient spaces, bases, and dimensions.
  • Perform advanced matrix reductions (Row Echelon, Normal PAQ form, LU Decomposition).
  • Analyze and solve linear systems using Rouché-Capelli consistency criteria and matrix factorizations.
  • Compute eigenvalues, eigenvectors, characteristic polynomials, and apply the Cayley-Hamilton Theorem.
  • Diagonalize matrices and classify real quadratic forms using Sylvester's Law of Inertia.
  • Construct matrix representations of linear transformations, verify the Rank-Nullity Theorem, and analyze vector space isomorphisms.

Course Units

Unit 1

Matrices and Systems of Equations

Special matrices (Hermitian, Unitary, Orthogonal, Idempotent, Nilpotent), Matrix Rank, Echelon Forms, Normal PAQ Form, Inversion, Systems of Equations, and LU Decomposition.

Study Unit 1
Unit 2

Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

Characteristic polynomial, Cayley-Hamilton Theorem, Minimal Polynomial, Algebraic vs Geometric Multiplicity, Matrix Diagonalization, and Quadratic Forms classification.

Study Unit 2
Unit 3

Vector Spaces

Groups & Fields, Vector Spaces, Subspaces, Quotient Spaces V/W, Linear Independence & Dependence, Linear Span, Basis, and Dimension theorems.

Study Unit 3
Unit 4

Linear Transformations

Linear Mappings, Kernel & Range, Rank-Nullity Theorem, Matrix Representation, Change of Basis Matrix, Inverse Transformations, and Vector Space Isomorphisms.

Study Unit 4
