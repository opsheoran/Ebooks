Unit IV: Linear Transformations

Linear mappings $T: V \to W$, Kernel $\text{Ker}(T)$, Range $\text{Im}(T)$, Rank-Nullity Theorem, Matrix Representation $[T]_{eta}^{\gamma}$, Change of Basis Matrix $P$, and Vector Space Isomorphisms $V \cong W$

4.1 Definition and Examples of Linear Transformations

A linear transformation is a structure-preserving mapping between vector spaces that respects vector addition and scalar multiplication. Linear transformations form the theoretical bridge connecting abstract vector spaces with concrete matrix operations.
Figure 4.1: Linear Transformation Mapping Domain V to Codomain W
Structure-preserving mapping T: V → W respecting vector addition T(u+v) = T(u)+T(v) and scalar multiplication T(c u) = c T(u).
Linear Transformation Definition
Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over the same field $F$. A function $T: V \to W$ is a linear transformation (or linear map) if for all $u, v \in V$ and all $c \in F$:
  1. $T(u + v) = T(u) + T(v)$ (Additivity)
  2. $T(c u) = c T(u)$ (Homogeneity)
Equivalently: $T(a u + b v) = a T(u) + b T(v)$ for all $a, b \in F$.

4.1 रेखीय रूपांतरण की परिभाषा (Linear Transformations)

एक फलन $T: V \to W$ Linear Transformation कहलाता है यदि यह जोड़ और अदिश गुणन को बनाए रखता है: $T(au + bv) = aT(u) + bT(v)$।

4.2 Kernel (Null Space) and Range (Image)

Figure 4.2: Kernel Ker(T) vs Range Im(T) Space Decomposition
Kernel Ker(T) ⊆ V mapped entirely to zero vector 0_W, while Range Im(T) ⊆ W contains all images T(v).
Kernel & Range Definitions
  • Kernel (Null Space) $\text{Ker}(T)$: $\text{Ker}(T) = \{v \in V \mid T(v) = 0_W\}$. Subspace of $V$. Dimension is Nullity $\text{nullity}(T)$.
  • Range (Image) $\text{Im}(T)$: $\text{Im}(T) = \{T(v) \in W \mid v \in V\}$. Subspace of $W$. Dimension is Rank $\text{rank}(T)$.

4.2 कर्नेल और परास (Kernel and Range)

Kernel $\text{Ker}(T)$: उन सभी $v \in V$ का समूह जो $0_W$ पर मैप होते हैं। Range $\text{Im}(T)$: सभी छवियों $T(v)$ का समूह।

4.3 Rank-Nullity Theorem

Theorem 4.1: Rank-Nullity Theorem (Dimension Theorem)
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space and $T: V \to W$ be a linear transformation. Then: $$ \text{rank}(T) + \text{nullity}(T) = \dim V $$ where $\text{rank}(T) = \dim(\text{Im}(T))$ and $\text{nullity}(T) = \dim(\text{Ker}(T))$.

4.3 रैंक-नुलिटी प्रमेय (Rank-Nullity Theorem)

यदि $V$ परिमित-आयामी (finite-dimensional) है, तो: $\text{rank}(T) + \text{nullity}(T) = \dim V$।

4.4 Matrix Representation of Linear Transformations

Let $eta = \{v_1, \dots, v_n\}$ be an ordered basis of $V$ and $\gamma = \{w_1, \dots, w_m\}$ be an ordered basis of $W$. The matrix representation $[T]_{eta}^{\gamma} \in M_{m \times n}(F)$ has $j$-th column equal to the coordinate vector $[T(v_j)]_{\gamma}$.

$$ [T(v)]_{\gamma} = [T]_{eta}^{\gamma} [v]_{eta} $$

4.4 रेखीय रूपांतरण का आव्यूह निरूपण (Matrix Representation)

आधारों $eta$ और $\gamma$ के सापेक्ष $T$ का आव्यूह $[T]_{eta}^{\gamma}$ होता है जिसके कॉलम $[T(v_j)]_{\gamma}$ होते हैं।

4.5 Change of Basis Matrix & Inverse Transformation

Let $P = [I]_{eta'}^{eta}$ be the change of basis matrix from $eta'$ to $eta$. Then for a linear operator $T: V \to V$:

$$ [T]_{eta'} = P^{-1} [T]_{eta} P $$

4.5 आधार परिवर्तन आव्यूह (Change of Basis Matrix)

नये आधार $eta'$ में रूपांतरण आव्यूह समरूपता (similarity) नियम का पालन करता है: $[T]_{eta'} = P^{-1} [T]_{eta} P$।

4.6 Isomorphism of Vector Spaces

Isomorphism Definition
A linear transformation $T: V \to W$ is an isomorphism if $T$ is bijective (one-to-one and onto). $V$ and $W$ are called isomorphic (denoted $V \cong W$).
Theorem 4.2: Fundamental Theorem of Finite-Dimensional Vector Spaces
Two finite-dimensional vector spaces $V$ and $W$ over field $F$ are isomorphic if and only if: $$ \dim V = \dim W $$ In particular, every $n$-dimensional vector space $V(F)$ is isomorphic to $F^n$.

4.6 सदिश समष्टियों की तुल्याकारिता (Isomorphism of Vector Spaces)

दो vector spaces $V \cong W$ तुल्याकारी (isomorphic) होते हैं यदि और केवल यदि $\dim V = \dim W$।

4.7 Solved Multiline Numerical Examples

Example 4.7.1: Kernel, Range, and Rank-Nullity Verification

Problem: Let $T: \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be defined by $T(x, y, z) = (x + 2y - z, y + z, x + 3y)$. Find a basis for $\text{Ker}(T)$, a basis for $\text{Im}(T)$, and verify the Rank-Nullity Theorem.

Step-by-Step Solution:

Step 1: Matrix Representation in Standard Basis:

$$ A = [T] = egin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 \ 0 & 1 & 1 \ 1 & 3 & 0 \end{bmatrix} $$

Step 2: Find Kernel $\text{Ker}(T)$: Solve $AX = 0$:

$$ R_3 \to R_3 - R_1 \implies egin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 \ 0 & 1 & 1 \ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \to R_3 \to R_3 - R_2 \implies egin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 \ 0 & 1 & 1 \ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} $$ $$ y + z = 0 \implies y = -z, \quad x + 2(-z) - z = 0 \implies x = 3z $$ $$ \text{Ker}(T) = \{z(3, -1, 1) \mid z \in \mathbb{R}\} \implies \text{Basis of Ker}(T) = \{(3, -1, 1)\}, \quad \text{Nullity}(T) = 1 $$

Step 3: Find Range $\text{Im}(T)$ and Rank:

Pivot columns are Col 1 and Col 2 of $A$: $\text{Basis of Im}(T) = \{(1, 0, 1), (2, 1, 3)\}$, $\text{Rank}(T) = 2$.

Step 4: Rank-Nullity Verification:

$$ \text{Rank}(T) + \text{Nullity}(T) = 2 + 1 = 3 = \dim(\mathbb{R}^3) \text{ (Verified!)} $$

4.8 Chapter End Exercises and Self-Assessment Bank

Part A: Multiple Choice Questions (20 MCQs)

Q1. If $T: V \to W$ is a linear transformation with $\dim V = 5$ and $\text{nullity}(T) = 2$, then $\text{rank}(T)$ is:
Correct Answer: (b) — $\text{rank}(T) = \dim V - \text{nullity}(T) = 5 - 2 = 3$.

Part B: Fill in the Blanks (20 Questions)

FIB 1. A linear transformation $T$ is one-to-one if and only if $\text{Ker}(T) = \{ \text{___________} \}$.
Answer: 0 — $\text{Ker}(T) = \{0\}$.

TF 1. Two finite-dimensional vector spaces are isomorphic if and only if they have the same dimension.
Answer: True — Fundamental Theorem of Finite-Dimensional Vector Spaces.
