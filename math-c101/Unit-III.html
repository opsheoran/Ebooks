Unit III: Vector Spaces

Groups, Fields, Vector space axioms, Subspaces, Direct sums $V = U \oplus W$, Quotient spaces $V/W$, Linear independence, Wronskian, Basis, and Dimension theorems

3.1 Groups and Fields

Field Definition
A field $(F, +, \cdot)$ is a set $F$ equipped with two binary operations (addition $+$ and multiplication $\cdot$) satisfying:
  1. $(F, +)$ is an abelian group with additive identity $0$.
  2. $(F \setminus \{0\}, \cdot)$ is an abelian group with multiplicative identity $1$.
  3. Multiplication distributes over addition: $a(b + c) = ab + ac$.
Standard fields in linear algebra: Real numbers $\mathbb{R}$, Complex numbers $\mathbb{C}$, Rational numbers $\mathbb{Q}$.

3.1 समूह और क्षेत्र (Groups and Fields)

एक **Field** $F$ (जैसे $\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}$) एक बीजीय संरचना (algebraic structure) है जिसमें जोड़ और गुणा के सभी 11 नियम लागू होते हैं।

3.2 Vector Spaces Definition and Axioms

Vector Space Definition
A vector space $V$ over a field $F$ (denoted $V(F)$) is a set $V$ equipped with vector addition $+: V \times V \to V$ and scalar multiplication $\cdot: F \times V \to V$ satisfying 8 axioms:
  • Vector Addition: Commutativity ($u+v=v+u$), Associativity ($u+(v+w)=(u+v)+w$), Zero vector ($v+0=v$), Additive inverse ($v+(-v)=0$).
  • Scalar Multiplication: $1v = v$, $a(bv) = (ab)v$, $a(u+v) = au + av$, $(a+b)v = av + bv$.

3.2 सदिश समष्टि की परिभाषा (Vector Space Definition)

Field $F$ पर एक **Vector Space** $V(F)$ सेट $V$ है जो 8 अक्षीय नियमों (axioms) को पूरा करता है।

3.3 Subspaces and Direct Sums

Theorem 3.1: Subspace Test
A non-empty subset $W \subseteq V$ is a subspace of $V(F)$ if and only if for all $u, v \in W$ and all $a, b \in F$: $$ a u + b v \in W $$
Figure 3.1: Direct Sum Decomposition V = U ⊕ W
Direct sum V = U ⊕ W where subspaces U and W intersect only at the zero vector {0}.

3.3 उप-समष्टि और प्रत्यक्ष योग (Subspaces & Direct Sums)

उप-समष्टि (Subspace) $W \subseteq V$ खुद एक vector space होता है। जब $U \cap W = \{0\}$, तो $V = U \oplus W$ (Direct Sum)।

3.4 Quotient Spaces V/W

Let $W$ be a subspace of $V$. The cosets $v + W = \{v + w \mid w \in W\}$ form the Quotient Space $V/W$.

Figure 3.2: Quotient Space V/W Coset Partitioning
Quotient space V/W partitioning vector space V into parallel affine cosets parallel to subspace W.
Theorem 3.2: Dimension of Quotient Space
If $V$ is finite-dimensional and $W$ is a subspace of $V$, then: $$ \dim(V/W) = \dim V - \dim W $$

3.4 भागफल समष्टि (Quotient Spaces V/W)

भागफल समष्टि $V/W$ के तत्व कोसेट $v + W$ होते हैं। आयाम सूत्र: $\dim(V/W) = \dim V - \dim W$।

3.5 Linear Span & Linear Combinations

The linear span $L(S)$ or $\text{span}(S)$ of a set of vectors $S = \{v_1, v_2, \dots, v_k\}$ is the set of all linear combinations $\sum a_i v_i$. $L(S)$ is always the smallest subspace of $V$ containing $S$.

3.5 रेखीय जनक और संयोग (Linear Span)

वेक्टरों $S$ के सभी सम्भव रेखीय संयोजनों (linear combinations) के समुच्चय को Linear Span $L(S)$ कहते हैं।

3.6 Linear Independence and Dependence

Linear Independence Definition
A set of vectors $\{v_1, v_2, \dots, v_k\}$ is linearly independent if the equation: $$ a_1 v_1 + a_2 v_2 + \dots + a_k v_k = 0 $$ forces all scalar coefficients $a_1 = a_2 = \dots = a_k = 0$. Otherwise, the set is linearly dependent.

3.6 रेखीय स्वतंत्रता और निर्भरता (Linear Independence)

वेक्टरों का समूह रेखीय रूप से स्वतंत्र (Linearly Independent) होता है यदि $a_1 v_1 + \dots + a_k v_k = 0$ केवल तभी संभव हो जब सभी गुणांक $a_i = 0$ हों।

3.7 Basis and Dimension

Basis Definition
A subset $\mathcal{B} \subseteq V$ is a basis of $V$ if:
  1. $\mathcal{B}$ is linearly independent.
  2. $\mathcal{B}$ spans $V$ ($\text{span}(\mathcal{B}) = V$).
The number of vectors in $\mathcal{B}$ is the dimension of $V$, denoted $\dim V$.
Theorem 3.3: Extension Theorem
Any linearly independent set in a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ can be extended to form a basis of $V$.

3.7 आधार और आयाम (Basis and Dimension)

आधार (Basis) $\mathcal{B}$ स्वतंत्र होता है और पूरे $V$ को जनित (span) करता है। $\mathcal{B}$ में वेक्टरों की संख्या को **Dimension** ($\dim V$) कहते हैं।

3.8 Solved Multiline Numerical Examples

Example 3.8.1: Subspace Verification Test

Problem: Test whether $W = \{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 \mid 2x - y + 3z = 0\}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$. If yes, find its dimension.

Step-by-Step Solution:

Let $u = (x_1, y_1, z_1), v = (x_2, y_2, z_2) \in W$ and $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$.

$$ 2x_1 - y_1 + 3z_1 = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad 2x_2 - y_2 + 3z_2 = 0 $$ $$ au + bv = (ax_1 + bx_2, ay_1 + by_2, az_1 + bz_2) $$ $$ 2(ax_1+bx_2) - (ay_1+by_2) + 3(az_1+bz_2) = a(2x_1-y_1+3z_1) + b(2x_2-y_2+3z_2) = a(0) + b(0) = 0 $$

Hence $au + bv \in W$, so $W$ is a subspace. Dimension $\dim W = 3 - 1 = 2$.

Example 3.8.2: Basis Extension

Problem: Extend the linearly independent set $S = \{(1, 1, 0), (0, 1, 1)\}$ to a basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$.

Step-by-Step Solution:

Consider standard basis vector $e_1 = (1, 0, 0)$. Test linear independence of $S \cup \{e_1\}$:

3.9 Chapter End Exercises and Self-Assessment Bank

Part A: Multiple Choice Questions (20 MCQs)

Q1. The intersection of any two subspaces of $V$ is always:
Correct Answer: (b) — Intersection of subspaces $U \cap W$ is always a subspace.
Q2. If $\dim V = n$ and $W$ is a subspace of dimension $m$, then $\dim(V/W)$ equals:
Correct Answer: (b) — $\dim(V/W) = \dim V - \dim W$.

Part B: Fill in the Blanks (20 Questions)

FIB 1. The dimension of the vector space $\mathbb{R}^n$ over $\mathbb{R}$ is ____________.
Answer: n — Standard basis has $n$ elements.

Part C: True / False Statements (20 Questions)

TF 1. The union of two subspaces is always a subspace.
Answer: False — Union $U \cup W$ is a subspace iff one is contained in the other.

Part E: Subjective Questions

Q1. Prove that the intersection of two subspaces $U$ and $W$ of $V$ is a subspace of $V$.
Let $u, v \in U \cap W$ and $a, b \in F$. Since $u, v \in U$, $au+bv \in U$. Since $u, v \in W$, $au+bv \in W$. Thus $au+bv \in U \cap W$.
