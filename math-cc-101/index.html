Course Preface

Linear Algebra and Matrix Theory forms the foundational pillar of modern mathematical sciences, statistics, data modeling, and engineering computations. This manual has been structured specifically for undergraduate students at CCS Haryana Agricultural University to master both conceptual rigor and hand-calculation techniques.

"Linear algebra is the language of modern mathematics. Mastering vector spaces, matrix representations, and spectral properties empowers students to solve complex mathematical and empirical problems with precision."

Official Course Syllabus

Course Code: MATH-CC-101  |  Title: Linear Algebra and Matrix Theory  |  Credits: 4 (4+0)

Unit I: Matrices & Systems of Linear Equations

Types of matrices: Symmetric, Skew-Symmetric, Hermitian, Skew-Hermitian, Orthogonal, Unitary, and Nilpotent. Elementary row and column operations, rank of a matrix, Row Echelon form, Reduced Row Echelon form. Normal form $PAQ$, inverse of matrix via EROs, LU decomposition. System of linear equations $AX = B$, consistency conditions, Gaussian elimination, and Gauss-Jordan method.

Unit II: Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & Quadratic Forms

Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, characteristic equation, characteristic polynomial. Properties of eigenvalues of symmetric, skew-symmetric, Hermitian, and unitary matrices. Cayley-Hamilton Theorem and applications in finding matrix powers and inverses. Minimal polynomial, diagonalizable matrices, quadratic forms, matrix of quadratic form, principal minor test ($D_1, D_2, D_3$), rank, index, signature, and definiteness classification.

Unit III: Vector Spaces & Inner Product Spaces

Vector space over a field, subspaces, subspace criterion theorem ($\alpha u + \beta v \in W$). Linear combinations, linear span $L(S)$, linear independence and dependence. Basis, dimension, dimension of sum $W_1 + W_2$, direct sum $W_1 \oplus W_2$. Quotient spaces, inner product spaces, Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, orthogonality, Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization process, and orthonormal basis.

Unit IV: Linear Transformations & Dual Spaces

Linear transformations, linear operators, zero transformation, identity operator. Kernel $\text{Ker}(T)$, image $\text{Im}(T)$, rank $\rho(T)$, nullity $\nu(T)$, Rank-Nullity Theorem ($\rho(T) + \nu(T) = \dim(V)$). Matrix representation $[T]_{B_1, B_2}$, change of basis similarity transformation $[T]_{B'} = P^{-1} [T]_B P$. Linear functionals, dual space $V^*$, dual basis $B^* = \{f_1, \dots, f_n\}$ ($f_i(v_j) = \delta_{ij}$), double dual $V^{**}$, annihilators $W^0$, and adjoint operators $T^*$.
