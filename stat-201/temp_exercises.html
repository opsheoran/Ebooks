Q8.Derive the normal equations for fitting a straight line Y = a + bX.

Q8.एक straight line Y = a + bX को fit करने के लिए normal equations derive करें।

Answer: We minimise E = Σ(yi - a - bxi)². Setting ∂E/∂a = 0 gives Σyi = na + bΣxi. Setting ∂E/∂b = 0 gives Σxiyi = aΣxi + bΣxi². These are the two normal equations.

Answer: We minimise E = Σ(yi - a - bxi)². Setting ∂E/∂a = 0 gives Σyi = na + bΣxi. Setting ∂E/∂b = 0 gives Σxiyi = aΣxi + bΣxi². These are the two normal equations.

Answer: Normal equations are the set of simultaneous linear equations obtained by setting the partial derivatives of the squared error sum to zero. They are called 'normal' because they represent the projection of the data onto the model space in a perpendicular (normal) sense.

Answer: Normal equations are the set of simultaneous linear equations obtained by setting the partial derivatives of the squared error sum to zero. They are called 'normal' because they represent the projection of the data onto the model space in a perpendicular (normal) sense.

Q13.State the normal equations for a second-degree parabola Y = a + bX + cX².

Q13.एक second-degree parabola Y = a + bX + cX² के लिए normal equations बताएं।

Q14.एक second-degree parabola को straight line की तुलना में कब prefer किया जाता है?

Q16.What is a residual? What does a zero sum of residuals indicate?

Q16.Residual क्या है? Residuals का zero sum क्या indicate करता है?

Answer: A residual is the difference between the observed value and the value predicted by the fitted model. A zero sum of residuals (Σe = 0) is a property of least square fits, indicating that the positive and negative errors cancel out.

Answer: A residual is the difference between the observed value and the value predicted by the fitted model. A zero sum of residuals (Σe = 0) is a property of least square fits, indicating that the positive and negative errors cancel out.

Q17.Why do we minimise the 'square' of deviations instead of just deviations?

Q17.हम सिर्फ deviations के बजाय deviations के 'square' को ही क्यों minimize करते हैं?

Answer: Minimising just deviations could result in a zero sum even for a very poor fit (due to signs cancelling). Using absolute deviations is mathematically difficult (not differentiable). Squared deviations are always positive and easier to handle using calculus.

Answer: Minimising just deviations could result in a zero sum even for a very poor fit (due to signs cancelling). Using absolute deviations is mathematically difficult (not differentiable). Squared deviations are always positive and easier to handle using calculus.

Q18.Describe the procedure for fitting a polynomial of degree k.

Q18.Degree k के polynomial को fit करने की procedure describe करें।

Answer: We write Y = a₀ + a₁X + ... + aₖXᵏ. We form k+1 normal equations by multiplying the model by 1, X, X², ..., Xᵏ and summing. The resulting linear system is solved for the k+1 coefficients.

Answer: We write Y = a₀ + a₁X + ... + aₖXᵏ. We form k+1 normal equations by multiplying the model by 1, X, X², ..., Xᵏ and summing. The resulting linear system is solved for the k+1 coefficients.

Q19.What is the difference between interpolation and extrapolation?

Q19.Interpolation और extrapolation में क्या अंतर है?

Answer: Interpolation is estimating values within the range of observed data. Extrapolation is estimating values outside the observed range. Extrapolation is generally more risky as the trend may not continue.

Answer: Interpolation is estimating values within the range of observed data. Extrapolation is estimating values outside the observed range. Extrapolation is generally more risky as the trend may not continue.

Q20.Observations की संख्या n, curve fitting की accuracy को कैसे प्रभावित करती है?
