Statistical Methods-II (STAT-M-201)

By Prof. O.P. Sheoran · Department of Mathematics & Statistics
Course Overview: Designed for B.Sc. II Year students, covering Correlation, Spearman's Rank Correlation, Curve Fitting by Least Squares, Linear Regression Analysis, and Multivariate/Trivariate Data Analysis.

📚 Course Units & Curriculum

Select any unit below to access interactive theory, derivations, SVG visualizations, solved numericals, and 120 practice questions (30 MCQs, 30 FIBs, 30 TFs, 30 Subjective Qs per unit).

UNIT I

Correlation & Rank Correlation

Bivariate distribution, Scatter diagram, Karl Pearson's correlation coefficient \(r\), change of origin and scale, limits of \(r\), bivariate frequency table, Spearman's rank correlation \(r_s\) with derivation and tied ranks formula.

8 Topics
120 Questions
Study Unit I
UNIT II

Curve Fitting & Least Squares

Principle of Least Squares, fitting of straight line \(Y=a+bX\), second-degree parabola \(Y=a+bX+cX^2\), power curve \(Y=aX^b\), exponential curves \(Y=ab^X\) and \(Y=ae^{bX}\), and logarithmic transformations.

8 Topics
120 Questions
Study Unit II
UNIT III

Linear Regression Analysis

Two lines of regression (\(Y\) on \(X\) and \(X\) on \(Y\)), regression coefficients \(b_{yx}, b_{xy}\) and their 5 fundamental properties, angle between regression lines (\(\tan \theta\)), standard error of estimate, and observed vs estimated values correlation.

8 Topics
120 Questions
Study Unit III
UNIT IV

Multivariate Data Analysis

Trivariate regression plane \(X_1 = a + b_{12.3} X_2 + b_{13.2} X_3\), properties of residuals, residual variance \(S_{1.23}^2\), coefficient of multiple correlation \(R_{1.23}\), partial correlation coefficient \(r_{12.3}\), and coefficient of determination.
